<h2 align="center">About</h2>

<p align="center">
Unity game developer with 2+ years of experience building and shipping commercial 2D and 3D mobile games.<br/>
I own the full cycle: gameplay systems, in-game UI, performance optimization, store release, and post-launch iteration.
</p>

- **Software engineering first.** Modular C# architecture, state handling, and object-oriented design behind every gameplay system, from timers and combo logic to tutorial flows and puzzle mechanics.
- **AI/ML in production.** On-device speech recognition with Whisper.cpp and Azure AI Foundry evaluating children's pronunciation in real time inside a shipped game, plus minimax with alpha-beta pruning for AI opponents.
- **Full stack range.** Comfortable beyond the engine with Flutter, React, Node.js, Supabase, Firebase, and Vercel, so I can take an idea from prototype to a live product.
- **Product engineering mindset.** Playtesting, tester feedback, and live metrics drive iteration. Features are judged by engagement and smoothness on real mobile devices.
